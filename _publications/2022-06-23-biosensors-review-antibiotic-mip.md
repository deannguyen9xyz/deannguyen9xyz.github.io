A review of the past decade of label-free sensors that use molecularly imprinted polymers to detect antibiotics in water, across optical, piezoelectric and electrochemical platforms. It compares the best-performing sensors for environmentally important antibiotics, covers computational selection of functional monomers and ways to amplify the signal, and outlines what is needed to move from proof of concept to commercial devices.
