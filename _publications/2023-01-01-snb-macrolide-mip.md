One sensor for a whole class of antibiotics, the macrolides. Imprinted with erythromycin, the polymer recognises the large lactone ring that all macrolides share, through reversible boronic acid–diol bonds and weaker non-covalent interactions. It responds about four times more strongly than a non-imprinted control to erythromycin, clarithromycin and azithromycin, with detection limits of 1.1–1.6 nM and 93–108 % recovery.
