A sensor for the fungicide azoxystrobin in water. The imprinted polymer was grown electrochemically on a thin-film metal electrode from two monomers, aniline and m-phenylenediamine, with azoxystrobin as the template. Detection limit 3.6 nM in tap water; selective for azoxystrobin, with 94–119 % recovery.
