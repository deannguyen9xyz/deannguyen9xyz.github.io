A sensor that measures the antibiotic ampicillin directly in water. A molecularly imprinted polymer on a ruthenium oxide electrode recognises ampicillin, and the ruthenium oxide itself provides the redox signal, so no added probe solution is needed. Detection limit 6 nM; 98–114 % recovery in pond water.
