Brings the three sensor papers above together. The thesis develops imprinted-polymer electrochemical sensors for single pollutants (the fungicide azoxystrobin and the antibiotic ampicillin) and for a whole antibiotic class (the macrolides), uses quantum-chemical modelling to choose functional monomers, and shows that a ruthenium oxide electrode can act as its own redox probe, so no added probe solution is needed.
